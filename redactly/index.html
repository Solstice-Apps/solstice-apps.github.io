Questions

Does Redactly upload my screenshots?

No. Screenshots and page text are processed inside the extension on your computer and are never sent anywhere. The only network request Redactly makes is checking a Pro license key with Polar, our payment provider.

Can it redact screenshots from other apps?

Yes. Take a screenshot with your system's shortcut, open Redactly and paste it. It reads the text in the image and hides what it finds. That works for terminals, chat apps, remote desktops and anything else on screen.

Will it catch everything?

It catches the common kinds of sensitive data reliably, and you always review the result before copying. Text in pasted images is read with on-device text recognition, so very small or blurry text can be missed. Drag over anything you want covered.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, plus Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store, like Edge and Brave. Versions for other browsers are planned.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Use the link in your purchase email, or the customer portal. Monthly plans can be canceled anytime and stay active until the end of the period.
