Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Redactly is a browser extension made by Solstice Apps ("we"). It hides sensitive information in screenshots. This policy explains what data Redactly handles and what happens to it. The short version: your screenshots and the text in them never leave your computer.

What Redactly handles, and where it goes

DataWhat happens to itShared with
Screenshots of the page you capture, and images you paste or drop in Processed in memory inside the extension on your computer, only while the editor is open. Never stored or uploaded. No one
Text on the page you capture, and text read from images Checked on your computer for sensitive information, then discarded. Never stored or uploaded. No one
Your settings (which kinds of data to hide, blur style, where the editor opens, your always-hide words) Saved in your browser's extension storage. If you use browser sync, your browser may sync them to your other devices. No one (your browser's own sync, if you use it)
Your Pro license key Saved in your browser's extension storage. Sent to Polar to check that it's valid, when you enter it and about every 3 days afterward. Polar (our payment provider)
A count of pasted screenshots read today (free plan) Saved in your browser's extension storage to apply the daily limit. Resets each day. No one

What Redactly does not do

  • It does not upload, store or transmit your screenshots, page content or browsing history.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • It does not run on pages until you capture one. It reads a page only at the moment you press the shortcut or the Capture button.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and does not use data for any purpose other than hiding sensitive information for you.

Purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Polar, which acts as merchant of record. When you buy, you give your name, email address and payment details to Polar, not to Redactly. Polar sends your license key by email and handles receipts, taxes and refunds. Polar's handling of that information is covered by Polar's privacy policy. We can see order details in Polar (such as your email address and purchase), which we use only to provide the product and support.

Browser permissions

PermissionWhy Redactly needs it
activeTabTo capture the tab you're on, only when you press the shortcut or click Capture.
scriptingTo find sensitive text on that page at the moment of capture, and to show the editor on top of it.
storageTo save your settings, license key and the free plan's daily count.
clipboardWriteTo copy the redacted image so you can paste it.
Access to api.polar.shTo check a Pro license key. Nothing else is sent.

Children

Redactly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
